# V60 Garage

A learn / diagnose / maintain app for one car: Kyle's 2016 Volvo V60 T5 Drive-E Premier. It runs on GitHub Pages as an installable app for iPhone and desktop, and also as a Claude artifact. Kyle directs the work and doesn't write code. Explain changes in plain words.

Read `docs/ROADMAP.md` before starting. It holds the backlog, the decisions so far, and open questions for Kyle. `docs/chats/` holds a digest of every claude.ai chat about the car (what Kyle said and decided, facts with sources, open questions), with personal details left out. **Since 2026-11-26 the app is an owner's reference, not a game** (Kyle: "not so gamified ... more of an informative thing"; he chose the "status board" mock-up, Design canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/FgGtaUHs7zq77Zm97YkTMe; plan and what was retired in ROADMAP, "Reference redesign"). The home (`car`, drawn by `src/js/modules/home.js`) shows what is wrong with the car now (Health's findings), what is coming due on Volvo's schedule against the service log, then eight section tiles. The bar has five roots: Home · Maintain (`maint`, home.js) · Fix (`trouble`) · Glovebox · Link. Every page, chapter, article, fact and Codebook label is open from the start: story.js forces `story.all = true` (the old "Show everything"), the manual marks no chapter new, locked or "pick up here", and nothing is announced. Know your car (`know`) holds the side-view figure; each point opens a view (hood, wheel, cabin, underside, walk-around) whose parts (items) are small manuals of chapters (Know, Check, Service, Diagnose, Upgrade). A part page holds all of its chapters as sections; each step opens in place with the Library article, the Training lesson, the DIY job or the troubleshooter inside it (`ch:` jumps land on the part page with that chapter's first undone step open). Retired from view but still in the code, with their saved data untouched: the path (`src/js/data/path.js`, `window.V60_PATH`), the story cases and scenes (`story.js`), the guided walkthrough (`guide.js`, intros off), the certification track's header chip and home strip (the G1 study and quizzes stay reachable from the Index as an optional shelf), Today's notes and Achievements. `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` describes the retired game. A new page needs a way in: a home tile, an `INDEX` group or an `ALSO` strip in manual.js; `tests/nav.js` fails otherwise.

## The car (public repo: never commit the VIN, plate or chassis number)
- 2016 V60 T5 Drive-E Premier, FWD, B4204T11 2.0 L turbo four, 240 hp / 258 lb-ft, Aisin TG-81SC 8-speed, start/stop, indirect tire monitor (no wheel sensors).
- Volvo bulletin codes: engine code 40, vehicle type 155.
- Bought about Aug 2025, as-is from an independent lot (Lams Austin Autos), at about 84,550 miles, with no service history. 91,357 miles on 2026-10-14 (cluster photo). Digital instrument cluster (a Premier item). Austin, TX (Travis County emissions testing). iPhone only.
- Tire pressure light is always on. Timing belt history is unknown.

## Rules for content
- Every fact needs a real source. Prefer Volvo's owner's manual, Volvo bulletins (NHTSA PDFs), NHTSA APIs and Volvo press releases. Sources live in `window.V60_SOURCES` (facts.js), or as `[title, url]` pairs.
- Mark anything unconfirmed as unconfirmed in the UI text. Never guess numbers, prices, the car's color or its options.
- Volvo's manual wins over forums and generic advice. Where Volvo's documents conflict, say so.
- No meta-commentary or praise in UI copy. Plain, direct sentences.

## Layout
- `src/` is the only place to edit. The root `index.html`, `version.json`, `app.css`, `js/`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest` and all of `dist-artifact/` are build output.
- `src/body.html` holds the core markup; `src/css/*.css` are concatenated, `app.css` first, then alphabetically (`manual.css` the manual's UI, `modules.css`, `theme.css` last: the workshop-manual dress for everything else). Fonts: Barlow Condensed (display), IBM Plex Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (numbers, labels), from `src/fonts.html`.
- `src/js/data/manual.js` is the manual's spine (views, items, chapters, steps; `caseOpens` only labels which case points at a chapter); `src/js/modules/manual.js` is its engine and UI (tabs `car` (the home; content from `window.V60_HOME.html`), `know`, `view`, `item`, `index`, `path` (retired); `compute()` opens every chapter because `story.all` is on; `window.V60_MANUAL`; the gate wrapper; no markers since 2026-11-26; photo views with pins under `mod("manual").views`). The part page (`renderItem`, also chapter 0 as item `"0"`) renders chapters as sections and steps as `<details>` that open in place: articles through `window.V60_KB.articleHtml`, lessons through `api.trainInto(containerId, missionId)` (core's Training renders into any container; `api.trainReset()` when leaving), DIY jobs through `window.V60_DIYUI.html/done`, troubleshooters with a small walker in manual.js; workbench steps (records, Live link, photos, bulletins) keep a Go button. Navigation: five roots in the bar (`SECTIONS` in core.js: `car` Home, `maint` Maintain, `trouble` Fix, `glove`, `live`). Settings (sync, backup, detail level) are their own page (`settings`, home.js), linked from the home and the Index; the sync chip's `opts:sync` jump opens it. Every other tab is hidden-unless-active and reached by jumps, the home's tiles, the Index page (`INDEX` groups), or the See also strips (`ALSO`: on each view, and under the titles of Glovebox, Link and Fix). The crumb bar (`#crumb`) keeps a trail: "← Back to …" retraces the path from any page, and a root clears it. Inner pages (anything but the home) get a one-row header (`body.inner`): the full dashboard (Health gauge, odometer) lives on the home only. A new page must go in `INDEX` (and `ALSO` where it fits); `tests/nav.js` fails if any page has no way in.
- `src/js/core.js` is the garage engine: Training (the Academy code, tab id `academy`, hidden from the nav and opened from story steps; it renders into `aui.root`, normally `acadBody`), bay maps, specs, health score, records, Data lab, simulator, backup. It exposes `window.V60` (the API) and `window.__v60` (test hook). The old XP totals still exist internally (`xpTotal`) but nothing shows them; `hud()` writes the track's readiness into the header chip.
- `src/js/data/track.js` is the certification track's data (G1 areas with question counts, part and fact mapping, pass bar) and `quiz.js` its question bank (original ASE-style questions, each with a source, area, rung and part; recall questions are built from V60_FACTS at run time). `src/js/modules/track.js` also renders a path study step (jump `study:D1-D13`) and starts a quiz from the path (jump `quiz:sus:1`); tasks, quizzes and the mock stay locked until the path reaches them, and a chapter set aside counts as hands-on done (`V60_MANUAL.settled`). It owns `window.V60_TRACK` (`ready()`, `areaReady(id)`, `rung(id)`, `passedG1()`, `strip()`, `hud()`, `areaOf(itemId)`) and `window.V60_PERK` (mech: any area at Owner; timeline: 3 records or receipts). Saved under `track`: `quiz{area:rung}`, `g1`. The story's finale needs `passedG1()`.
- `src/js/data/` holds `facts.js` (V60_SOURCES and V60_FACTS, which drive the Library, Cards and the quizzes' recall questions), `articles.js` (V60_ARTICLES; body lines use "## " for headings and "- " for bullets) and `toolprices.js` (V60_TOOL_PRICES: store prices for the Glovebox tool inventory, each from a fetched retailer page, with the date checked; the owner's own prices live in `glove.price`).
- `src/js/modules/*.js` is one feature per file. Each registers itself:
  `(window.V60_MODULES = window.V60_MODULES || []).push({ id, tabs:[{id, section, label, order, hidden?, html}], init(api), render(api), clean(v), xp(api), actions(api, list), today(api) -> [{order, html}], jump(api, kind, id) })`
  - Sections: `today`, `learn`, `car`, `check`, `data`.
  - Saved data lives under `api.mod(id)` / `api.setMod(id, v)`. `clean(v)` must validate it.
  - Navigation: `data-jump="tab:<tabId>"`, or a module's own kind such as `kb:<articleId>`, `diy:<jobId>`, `tree:<id>`, `tsb:<id>`, `story:<id>`, `ask:<text>`.
- `src/js/modules/live.js` is generated. Edit `tools/gen/live_extra.js`, `tools/gen/live_claude.js`, `tools/gen/live_auto.js` (Connect by itself), `tools/gen/live_native.js` (inside the V60 iPhone app: the page uses the app's adapter link and its readings fill the gauges; `tests/native.js`) or `tools/gen/mklive.py`, then run `python3 tools/gen/mklive.py`. It ports `src/link-legacy.html` (the original V60 Link). Element ids are prefixed `lk-`.
- `ios/` is the V60 iPhone app (see `ios/README.md`): native Bluetooth to the adapter in the background, drive recording, the Claude live link. Its drive reports are built on Kyle's PC by `bridge/drives.js`.
- **Claude live** (2026-11-26): `tools/gen/live_claude.js` is the phone side (card under Connect on Link, off until paired; settings in localStorage `v60-claude`, never synced); `bridge/` is the PC side (`bridge/SETUP.md`, `bridge/CLAUDE.md`). Since 2026-11-27 it is an always-on background service (`run.js` keeps `service.js` running from Windows sign-in, restarting it when bridge code changes; no window, no /mcp): `hub.js` holds the phone link, and each event runs Claude Code headless (`claude -p`, the day's conversation resumed, car tools via `tools.js`, notebook `bridge/data/notes/car.md`). `server.js` is the older channel window, kept for interactive use and `tests/claude.js`; `tests/service.js` tests the service. Every command that could change the car needs Kyle's tap on the phone (`carWrite` in both `live_claude.js` and `bridge/lib.js`; keep the two rules identical). The Allow card and the message toast are moved to `document.body` so they show over any screen.
- To add a feature: create `src/js/modules/<name>.js`, add it to `MODULES` in `tools/build.py`, put its styles in `src/css/modules.css`, and give its tab a way in (a home tile, an `INDEX` group or an `ALSO` strip in manual.js).
- **Recorded drives** (2026-11-26): `src/js/modules/drives.js` owns `window.V60_DRIVES`. It reads `https://<pc>/drives/summary.json` from Kyle's PC (built by `bridge/drives.js` from the iPhone app's drives; reachable only over his Tailscale; `<pc>` is `window.V60_NATIVE.pc` inside the iPhone app, else `host` in localStorage `v60-claude`), caches it in localStorage `v60-drives` (never synced), and shows nothing when there is no address or answer. Core's `odoEst()` (`api.odoEst()`) is the entered odometer (`state.odo`, with its time `state.odoAt`) plus miles from drives that started after it; `statusFor` and `dueWithin` use it and mark miles "(estimated)". The entered reading is never overwritten. Maintain shows the Recorded drives card (estimate, last drive, this week, estimated mpg, links to the PC's reports, emissions readiness from the newest drive that read it); the Glovebox shows readiness next to the inspection date; Health lists a lit check-engine light or more than one monitor not ready (40 CFR 85.2222). `tests/drives.js` mocks the PC. Volvo's 2016 schedule PDF (`V60_SOURCES.ms`) opens blank in some iPhone browsers, so home.js turns every link to it into a jump to `vsched` ("Volvo's 2016 schedule"): the rows for this engine word for word with printed page numbers and footnotes (read from the PDF 2026-11-27), then the PDF itself (`data-direct`). Maintain's "Volvo's note" quotes the row.
- **Problem cases** (2026-11-27): `src/js/modules/cases.js` (Fix > Problem cases, tab `cases`) is for mechanical problems no computer reports (noises, vibration, pulling, brakes, clunks, leaks, tire wear). Kyle describes what he notices or sends a photo (through `api.shrinkPhoto`, then JPEG); it is posted to `https://<pc>/drives/case` (`bridge/drives.js` saves it under `bridge/data/cases/` and wakes Claude with `symptom` / `photo`). Claude keeps one case file per problem in `bridge/data/notes/cases.json` (schema and method in `bridge/CLAUDE.md`, "Mechanical problems"); `summary.json` carries it and `drives.js` validates it (`cleanCases`). Cases marked caution or stop reach Home and Health. `tests/cases.js`, and `tests/service.js` for the PC side.
- **Page cleanup** (2026-11-27, Kyle approved https://claude.ai/artifact/QtDuvWWwRPnwHXBTm2VSCw): one page per job. `src/js/modules/tidy.js` moves sections whole (same element ids, so core keeps driving them): the service log, oil watch and calendar reminders into Maintenance (`#mtLog`; the Service log page's own "What's due" stays behind, hidden), the code lookup and code log into Fix (`#trCodes`, with Ask about my car), Back up and restore into Settings. `window.V60_ALIAS` (read by core's `showTab`) sends retired pages to their new home: `records`, `diag`, `health` (Home), `engine` (the bay view in Know your car), `bay` (Know your car), `sim` (Data lab), `cards` (Library). Part pages show your condition rating for the matching bay part (`rateHtml` in manual.js; core's `data-rate` buttons work anywhere). My V60 is "This car", linked at the top of Know your car. Index groups: Your car, Maintain, Fix & diagnose, Learn, Tools (optional: Data lab, G1), Settings. Home tiles: Maintenance, Plan & shop, Know your car, Fix & diagnose, Bulletins & recalls, Glovebox, Fuel & costs, Library. The retired panels stay in the page, unlinked, with their data; `tests/nav.js` lists them as RETIRED and checks every redirect.
- **Plan & shop** (2026-11-27, Kyle chose mock-up A + the by-need switch, https://claude.ai/artifact/MPp5kSV6sckB5tmLhZpS52): `src/js/modules/shop.js`, tabs `shop` (the five sections of the car with Needed / Recommended / Only if needed / Upgrades groups, a "By section · By need" switch, an item view with four ways: check it yourself, do it yourself only with a sourced step-by-step, have a shop do it with what to ask for, ask Claude) and `plan` (DIY and shop jobs, priced-so-far total, shop-quote text, Log it done, ask Claude to check the plan). Saved in mod "shop" `{plan: {id: "diy"|"shop"}}`. The catalog `src/js/data/shop.js` only places items and points at existing data (core ITEMS/COSTS via `api.COSTS`, Volvo's printed rows via `V60_HOME.vrow`, V60_UPGRADES, V60_DIY, manual chapters); never put an unsourced price or fact there. `shop:<id>` jumps to an item. Ask Claude posts to `https://<pc>/drives/ask` (the PC session's route); without it the question is shown to copy. `tests/shop.js`.
- `sync.js` owns `window.V60_SYNC`. Core calls `changed()` after every local save, and sync uses `api.replaceState`, `api.snapshot`, `api.blobGet` and `api.blobPut`. The token lives in localStorage key `v60-sync`, never in the synced state. `ui.js` draws the header pulse strip and handles `data-speak` read-aloud. `scan.js` owns `window.V60_SCAN.process(file, kind)`: on-device reading (Tesseract.js 5.1.1 from jsDelivr with a pinned integrity hash, as is pdf.js in docs.js; cached by the service worker in `v60-ocr-2`) and blacking out personal details before any photo is stored or synced. ## Build, test, deploy
- Build: `python3 tools/build.py`. It writes the root files and `dist-artifact/`, and bumps the service worker version so installed apps show "Update available".
- Tests (Playwright). On Kyle's Windows PC (and anywhere with `npm install` done): `npm test` (= `node tests/run.js`) builds, then runs every test in Chromium and in WebKit dressed as an iPhone 13 (Bluefy's engine; `tests/engine.js` swaps the browser, serves the repo on 127.0.0.1 and gives each context a persistent profile, because WebKit's private contexts can't store Blobs in IndexedDB). Arguments: test names, `--chromium`/`--webkit`, `--no-build`. On Windows use `python`, not `python3`. In the cloud session, one test at a time:
  `PLAYWRIGHT_PATH=/opt/npm-tools/node_modules/playwright CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium node tests/regression.js`
  `tests/regression.js` checks every tab on phone, desktop and dark: no errors, no horizontal overflow, no broken `tab:` jumps. Every test sets exit code 1 on a failure.
  `tests/features.js` exercises Live link (simulated car), Bulletins and Upgrades. `tests/sync.js` exercises cross-device sync against a mocked GitHub API. `tests/ble.js` runs Live link against a mocked Bluetooth adapter that drops the first link (as the OBDLink CX can while iOS pairs). `tests/auto.js` plays Connect by itself against a mocked adapter that sleeps with the car off (no tap, no picker, screen kept on while connected, a manual Disconnect respected, a silent adapter given up after 20 s). `tests/scan.js` checks the redaction rules and reads a synthetic receipt end to end (needs jsDelivr reachable). `tests/odo.js` reloads the app four times to check saved data is never overwritten at startup. `tests/tire.js` reads a synthetic tire sidewall (DOT date, size) end to end. `tests/photos.js` saves a photo and checks the record and the picture survive reloads. `tests/update.js` checks the "new version is ready" bar works without a service worker (Bluefy has none). `tests/nav.js` checks every page has a way in and a way back (map button, index, see-also, crumb trail) and that the bay map faces the right way. `tests/tools.js` checks the Glovebox tool tiers, store prices and the owner's price override, and the ASE article. `tests/track.js` checks the optional G1 shelf: the question bank's shape, hands-on-first quiz gating, an area quiz end to end, the Owner rung, the mock G1 with its clock and score report, and no readiness chip or path card. `tests/claude.js` starts the real bridge over MCP stdio with a test client standing in for Claude and drives the simulated car through it (needs `npm install` in `bridge/`; skips otherwise). `tests/manual.js` exercises the reference app: the status-board home, every chapter and page open with no markers, steps opening in place (lesson, article, troubleshooter), the Go/back crumb, saved progress across a reload, and a photo as a view with draggable points. (The path, story and guide tests were retired with the game layer, 2026-11-26.) Tests set `window.__V60_TEST = true`, which stops the story prologue from auto-playing.

## UI
- Design tokens are in `src/css/app.css` (`--bg` paper, `--panel`, `--ink`, `--line`, `--new` amber which only ever means new or where you left off, `--done`, `--teal` for instrument traces). Square corners, hairlines, no shadows. Live link and the Data lab keep their instrument faces. Light and dark themes are both required.
- Phone first: Kyle uses the app only held upright. 390 px wide with no sideways scroll; `tests/portrait.js` fails on text under 11 px (drawings included, after they shrink), taps under 32 px, or sideways scrolling. Draw new figures tall, not wide; tables stack into cards on phones by themselves. Bottom bar on phone (Home · Maintain · Fix · Glovebox · Link), side rail on desktop. Figures are 358 wide schematic SVGs in `manual.js` (`FIG`); the owner's photo replaces a figure once pinned.
- With every feature, re-check that its screen isn't crowded. Move secondary material into `<details>`, and prefer one clear next action per card.
- Toggle groups use `.seg` with `aria-pressed`. Pills are `ok`, `watch`, `concern`/`bad`, and `info`/`none`.
- Files out of the app go through `window.V60_FILE.save(name, text, type)` (ui.js), never a bare `<a download>`: Bluefy on iPhone cannot download, cannot print, and its share sheet drops attached files and passes only the title, so the helper always includes the content as text. Copy buttons need a real selection on a visible box (`V60_FILE` does not cover copying; see the adapter log in link-legacy.html). Print buttons need a share-as-text twin (`V60_FILE.textOf(el)`).

## Claude ↔ Gemini handoff protocol
Kyle alternates between Claude and Gemini when usage limits hit. Neither AI can talk to the other, so state moves through one Markdown file.
- **"Initiate Handoff Protocol"** means: stop development immediately and produce the handoff:
  1. Rewrite `docs/HANDOFF_STATE.md` with three sections: `## 1. Current Active Objective`, `## 2. Immediate Next Steps` (precise, prioritized), and `## 3. Pending Logic & Blockers` (errors, hardware test parameters, design choices under evaluation).
  2. Run `python3 tools/handoff.py`. It writes `HANDOFF.md` (gitignored) with those sections plus `## 4. Complete Codebase`: every source file in full, copied byte for byte by the script (never typed out by hand, so nothing is truncated or replaced with placeholders), plus a manifest with SHA-256 prefixes and the icons in base64.
  3. Commit and push the source changes, then send `HANDOFF.md` to Kyle as a downloadable file.
- Build outputs (root `index.html`, `js/`, `sw.js`, `link.html`, `dist-artifact/`) are deliberately left out. `tools/build.py` regenerates them from `src/`, and the GitHub Action `.github/workflows/build.yml` runs that build on every push to `src/` or `tools/`.
- **Always-ready copy:** `.github/workflows/handoff.yml` rebuilds `HANDOFF.md` after every push to `main` and force-pushes it as the only file on the `handoff` branch (https://github.com/bmcdoorz94/V60-Link/blob/handoff/HANDOFF.md). A session can be cut off by a usage limit without warning, so keep `docs/HANDOFF_STATE.md` current and push finished work in small commits; the branch then always holds a usable handoff.
- **Receiving a handoff back** (Kyle uploads a .md from Gemini): run `python3 tools/apply_handoff.py <file> --dry-run`, review it, then run without `--dry-run`. It writes every `### \`path\`` + code block as a full-file replacement, refuses build outputs and paths outside the repo, handles `## Deleted files`, writes sections 1–3 to `docs/HANDOFF_STATE.md`, and prints any `## Edits to apply` (find-and-replace edits for files too large to return whole, such as core.js) for you to apply. Then build, run the tests, review the diff for accuracy problems and placeholders, and commit.
