Companion to V60 Garage

V60 Link

Talks to your car through a Bluetooth LE OBD-II adapter. It can read codes, freeze frames and emissions readiness, record live data, and clear codes with safeguards. The results export to V60 Garage.

Connect

Not connected
Before you connect
  1. Plug the adapter into the OBD-II port under the dashboard on the driver's side.
  2. Turn the ignition on. The engine can be off to read codes, but it must be running for live data.
  3. Do not pair the adapter in your phone's Bluetooth settings. This page finds it directly.
  4. Tap Connect and pick the adapter from the list. Its name is often OBDII, OBDBLE, IOS-Vlink, OBDLink CX or similar.
  5. Close any other OBD app first, because only one app can talk to the adapter at a time.
  6. OBDLink CX: it accepts a new pairing only during the first 5 minutes after it powers up. If it does not show up, unplug it, plug it back in, and connect right away.

Health check

Reads the VIN, the check-engine light, stored, pending and permanent codes from every module that answers, the freeze frame, and emissions readiness. Engine off with ignition on is fine.

Summary

    Codes

      Freeze frame

      A snapshot the engine computer saved at the moment the first code set.

      Emissions readiness

      Self-tests the computer runs during normal driving. A monitor that is "not ready" has not finished since codes were last cleared or the battery was disconnected.

      Live data

      Pick channels, start recording, and drive. Fewer channels give a faster update rate. The recording saves as a CSV that V60 Garage's Data lab reads directly. Have a passenger run it, or set it up before you drive.

      Clear codes

      Clearing codes turns the light off, but it does not fix anything. It also erases the freeze frame and resets every readiness monitor to "not ready." Until you drive enough for the monitors to finish, the car can fail a Texas emissions inspection. If the fault is still there, the light comes back.

      Unlocks only after a health check in this session, so the codes and freeze frame are saved first.

      Adapter conversation

      Every command sent and every reply, for troubleshooting.
