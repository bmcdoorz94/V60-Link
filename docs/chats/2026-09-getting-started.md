## Facts established (all now in the app)
- Engine B4204T11, 2.0 L four-cylinder turbo (Drive-E), 240 hp at 5,600 rpm, 258 lb-ft from 1,500 to 4,500 rpm, bore 82 mm, stroke 93.2 mm. Source: the 2016 V60 owner's manual Kyle uploaded (MY16 web edition, TP 20238). In the app: facts f-engine, f-hp, f-tq, f-bore.
- Front-wheel drive, 8-speed automatic. In the app: f-drive and the transmission article.
- The owner's manual is the generic MY16 V60 edition, not tied to one VIN. Volvo keeps the maintenance intervals in a separate Warranty and Service Records booklet, and the oil spec on a label under the hood. In the app: a-schedule, and the hood-label row on 1.1 Oil.

## Said in that chat, corrected or not used since
- "In 2016, T5 AWD meant the 2.5 L five-cylinder with a 6-speed" was said without a source. Volvo's "2016 V60: What's New" release lists the V60 T5 AWD as "Model discontinued" for 2016 (https://mb.cision.com/Main/23622/4196507/3563273.pdf). Not in the app.
- Oil "usually 0W-30 or 5W-30" was generic. Volvo's manual and Kyle's hood label give ACEA A5/B5 5W-30, with Volvo 0W-20 also approved (1.1 Oil in the app).
- The oil-consumption extended warranty (8 years or 100,000 miles) was raised here. The app's oil-program article covers it from Volvo's own documents and notes it has very likely expired on this car by age.
- Forum and YouTube suggestions (SwedeSpeed, ChrisFix and others) and the list of "known weak points" (A/C, struts, electrics) had no sources and were not used.
