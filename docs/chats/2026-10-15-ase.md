## Facts given, with sources (now in the app)
- ASE is a voluntary certification, not a license: pass a test and have matching work experience; credentials last five years. Tiers: Entry-Level (students), Professional tests (A1 to A9, G1 and others), Master (a full series, A1 to A8 for autos), Advanced Level L1 to L4.
- 2026 fees: $34 registration per order plus $62 per general test and $124 per Advanced Level test; G1 alone $96; A1 to A8 in one order $530; 90 days to test; in person at Prometric centers.
- Experience categories ASE lists; passing without them gives a score report, not a certification.
- Sources: ASE, Register Now (https://ase.com/tests/register-now/) and Work Experience (https://ase.com/tests/work-experience/). In the app: the ASE article (a-ase).
- ASE's free study guides work as a map of the car's systems, with the V60 as the reference object. This became the certification track and the G1 study entries.
